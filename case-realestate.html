The Before & After

Before Echofied

  • Leads sat for an average of 4 hours before an agent called them.
  • Agents spent 2 hours every evening manually updating Follow Up Boss.
  • Zillow leads were often lost to competitors who called faster.
  • Showings were booked via phone tag and messy text threads.

After Echofied

  • AI agent texts new leads within 60 seconds, qualifies them, and answers HOA/property questions.
  • Agent checks Google Calendar and books the showing directly on the spot.
  • CRM (Follow Up Boss) is updated automatically with full conversation logs.
  • Agents only step in when the lead is ready for a contract or a complex negotiation.

The Technology Stack

System Role How Echofied Connects
Follow Up Boss Real Estate CRM API Integration. Reads new leads, writes conversation notes & tags.
Zillow Premier Agent Lead Generation Webhook. Triggers the AI the second a new lead fills out a form.
Google Calendar Scheduling API Integration. AI checks availability and creates the calendar event.
Twilio SMS & Voice API. Acts as the phone number the AI uses to text and call.

The Automation Workflow

  1. Trigger: A lead submits a "Request a Tour" form on Zillow for 14 Alder Lane.
  2. Action: Echofied agent texts the lead: "Hi, this is Sarah with Hearthline. I see you're interested in 14 Alder Lane! It's a 3-bed, $705k. Would you like to see it this Saturday?"
  3. Qualification: The lead replies "Yes, Saturday at 11 AM." The AI answers 2 more questions about the HOA.
  4. Execution: AI checks the agent's Google Calendar, finds 11 AM open, books the event, and confirms via SMS.
  5. Logging: AI writes the entire transcript, the lead's budget, and the showing time into Follow Up Boss.
