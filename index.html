What does Echofied do?

Echofied is an AI automation platform that researches, creates, launches and improves your business workflows inside your rules. Every change to your systems is previewed and confirmed first.

Do I need coding skills to use Echofied?

No. The platform is designed for operators and founders. You describe what you want in plain English, and the AI builds the workflow and connects the tools.

Which tools and services can I connect?

We integrate with Salesforce, HubSpot, Stripe, Xero, Shopify, Slack, and any tool with an open API or standard database connection. We also support legacy systems via SFTP and ODBC.

How secure is my data?

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Access is scoped per user, PII is redacted before storage, and every read/write action is logged with its source for full auditability.

Can Echofied scale with my team?

Yes. You can invite unlimited team members, set granular permission levels, and manage multiple client workspaces across different regions.

How quickly can I get started?

Most clients go live in 2 to 4 weeks, depending on the complexity of their workflows and the number of integrations required.
