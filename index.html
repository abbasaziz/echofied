How it works

Four steps. No black boxes.

We plug into the tools you already use, run everything inside guardrails, and hand the exceptions to your team.

  1. 01

    Connect

    We link your inbox, phone number, calendar, and CRM — Sheets, GoHighLevel, HubSpot, Salesforce, or anything with an API.

        FAQ

        The questions that matter.

        Who owns the code and the data?

        You do. Every workflow, prompt, integration, and dataset lives in your accounts. If we ever part ways, everything keeps running exactly as it is.

        How fast can an AI receptionist go live?

        Receptionist deployments usually go live in 3 to 7 days. Custom ERP or CRM builds take 2 to 4 weeks depending on scope.

        What if the agent gets something wrong?

        Every action runs inside guardrails you set. Anything ambiguous or outside your policy is routed to a human for approval, and every decision is logged with a source.

        Which CRMs do you connect to?

        GoHighLevel, HubSpot, Salesforce, Pipedrive, Zoho, and any CRM with an API. For simpler operations, we connect directly to Google Sheets.

        Is this secure?

        Yes. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, access is scoped per user, and PII is redacted before storage. We can sign a DPA and follow your compliance requirements.

        Do you work with companies outside the US?

        Yes. We support clients across the US, EU, and UK, with voice agents fluent in English, Spanish, French, German, and more.
